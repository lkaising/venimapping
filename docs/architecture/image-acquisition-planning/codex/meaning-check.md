### Pre-existing inconsistencies

- The integrated §5 text at 335–502 matches `docs/architecture/image-acquisition-planning/codex/sections/data-plane.md:1-166` apart from its heading and source note. The integrated control-plane freshness explanation at 1000–1003 already says the caller skips a frame if it took its sequence before `subscribe`. The data-plane draft at 134–136 permits a frame numbered after the `R` read and before the queue link, if its push follows the link. The take-loop predicate `receive_seq <= R` at 980 matches the data plane. Apply that predicate to the explanation, and allow an in-flight frame to fail the cutoff even if it is the only pending frame.
