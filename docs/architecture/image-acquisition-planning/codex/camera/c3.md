# Camera check c3

## 1. Question

Answer R4 and T8 for register entry `d18` on 2026-11-23. Use the Allied Vision Alvium G1-130 VSWIR at `/vimbax_camera`, with full-sensor Mono8, free run, and `autostream=0`.

1. While stopped, what access mode and range does `AcquisitionFrameRate` report after `AcquisitionFrameRateEnable` is true?
2. Does a write of 30.0 Hz succeed, and what value does the camera read back?
3. After `stream_start`, what final rate, window, and interval limits does `ros2 topic hz` report? Is the rate within 29 to 31 Hz?

## 3. Result

1. [F] The camera reported Mono8 at 1296 x 1032. Before the first write, the enable was false and the rate read 69.71556091308594 Hz (steps 2 to 6).
2. [F] With the enable true and the stream stopped, the rate was readable and writable. The reported range contained 30.0 Hz, and the service reported no available increment (steps 7 to 9).
3. [F] The 30.0 Hz write succeeded. The camera read back 30.000001907348633 Hz (steps 10 and 11).
4. [F] The last rate was 30.010 Hz, within 29 to 31 Hz. The window held 555 intervals, with a minimum of 0.032 s and a maximum of 0.034 s (step 15).
5. [F] Both restore writes succeeded. The final readback matched the original rate and enable values (steps 16 to 20).

## 5. Limits

1. [F] The rate came from one camera and one 20 s topic command. The intervals measure delivery to the subscriber.
2. [A] The check used the runner's `autostream=0` setup and the free-run trigger state from c2. This check relied on those settings (`docs/architecture/image-acquisition-planning/claude/camera/c2.md:73-78`).
3. [Q] Rate writability during acquisition and profile readback after a settings file load or reconnect remain untested here.
