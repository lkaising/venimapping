## Task: take positions on the design choices

Work alone. The other planner does the same task from the same checkpoint. You
cannot see its positions until both are done. Do not look for them.

Read `{P}/coordinator/facts.md`, both traces, any camera check results, and
v1. Then write `{P}/{tool}/positions.md`. For each design choice in facts.md,
write these parts:

1. Position. The option that you recommend, in one or two sentences.
2. Evidence. The citations and facts that support the position.
3. Rejected options. Each other option, and the reason you reject it.
4. What would change your mind. A specific fact, measurement, or requirement.
5. Cost to reverse. Low, medium, or high, and one sentence about what changes
   if the choice is wrong.

Be specific. "Use a bounded queue" is not a position. Give the bound, the drop
rule, and the owner of the queue.

Then comment on each working assumption in facts.md. Say if you accept it, and
why.
