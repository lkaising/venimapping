## Task: argue dispute {issue}

Work alone. The other planner argues the same dispute from the same
checkpoint. You cannot see its argument until both are done.

Read entry {issue} in `{P}/register.md` or `{P}/issues.md`. Also read the
facts, both traces, and both positions files. Then write
`{P}/{tool}/disputes/{issue}-position.md` with these parts:

1. Recommendation. One option, stated so that a drafter can apply it.
2. Evidence. Use only citations, documented driver or SDK behavior, and
   measurable consequences. Style preferences do not count.
3. The other side. State the strongest case for the other option. Then say why
   it loses.
4. What evidence would change your recommendation.
5. Fallback. Give a working assumption that both planners could draft against
   if the judges disagree. Say what depends on it and the cost to reverse it.

You may change your earlier position. If you do, say so.
